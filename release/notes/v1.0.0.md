## Agent Setu 1.0.0 — first pilot release

A free, private, offline app to keep track of customers, renewals, maturities and commission for PLI, RPLI and Post Office savings schemes.

**What's in it**
- Hindi and English; customers, add business with the expected commission shown instantly, month-wise commission ledger.
- Reminders for premiums, RD instalments, maturities (30/15/7/1 days before) and follow-ups; one notification each morning.
- TD, RD and MIS calculators and cards for 10 schemes, shareable on WhatsApp as images.
- PIN / fingerprint lock, password-protected backup and restore.
- Commission and interest rates are editable. The bundled rates are samples — verify them against the latest orders.
