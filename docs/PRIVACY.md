# Privacy notice / गोपनीयता सूचना

> Linked from Settings → About in the app. A practical notice written by the developer, not legal advice.

**Effective date:** 28-09-2026 · **App:** Agent Setu 1.1.4 and later · **Contact:** the Agent Setu
users group, or [GitHub issues](https://github.com/rahulranjan-dev-py/Agent-Setu/issues)

## English

**1. Who we are.** Agent Setu is a free, independent, unofficial app made by an individual
developer. It is not an India Post / Department of Posts / IPPB product.

**2. What the app stores.** Only what you type in: your profile (staff type, designation, office,
division, agent codes), your customers (name, mobile, village, optional date of birth, notes), the
policies and accounts you track (product, amounts, dates and, only if you type it in, the policy or
account number, which the app shows in full only on that policy's own screen and masks to the
**last 4 digits** everywhere else), your commission records (including the receipts and incentive statements
you enter), targets and reminders. The policy/account number is never required. The app has **no
field** for Aadhaar, PAN or bank account numbers and asks for no departmental login.

**3. Where it is stored.** Only on your phone, in an encrypted database. The developer never
receives it. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs and needs no internet connection except
to check for a new version and, only when you tap **Download**, to fetch that new version.

**4. What leaves your phone, and only when you choose.**
- *Backup file:* a password-protected file that you save or share yourself.
- *Scheme cards and messages:* images or text you choose to share on WhatsApp or by call.
- *Error report:* when you tap "Send error report", the app shows you the report first. It contains
  the app version, Android version, phone model and the technical error. It contains **no customer
  data**. You choose whether and how to send it.
- *Update check:* each time the app opens or comes to the front (at most once every 15 minutes) it
  reads a public `version.json` file from GitHub. When you tap **Download**, it fetches the new APK
  from the app's GitHub release page. These are the app's only internet uses; nothing about you,
  your phone or your customers is sent.
- *Calculator results:* images you choose to share; they contain no customer data.

**5. Your customers' data.** You are responsible for having your customers' consent to record
their details and contact them. The app asks you to confirm consent when you add a customer.

**6. Your choices.** You can edit or delete any customer (their details, their policies' numbers and
your follow-up notes are wiped at once; commission you already earned stays in your ledger without the name), and use **Settings → Delete all my data**
to erase everything. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data. Backup files you saved elsewhere
must be deleted by you. You can lock the app with a PIN (and fingerprint, face or your phone's lock); only scrambled forms of
the PIN and of its recovery code are kept.

**7. Permissions.** Notifications (for reminders) is the only one you are asked for. The app also
has fingerprint / face access (only if you turn on that unlock) and background-scheduling
permissions for daily reminders; none of these reads your personal data. When you tap **Download** on
an update, the app fetches the new APK from the app's GitHub release page, checks it against the
published checksum and that it is signed with the same key as your copy, then asks Android to install
it; Android asks you once to allow installs from Agent Setu. Nothing is sent. The PIN and recovery-code
screens cannot be captured in screenshots, and the app is hidden in the Recents thumbnail on Android 13+. No SMS, call-log, contacts (unless you choose to import in a later version),
location or storage permissions.

**8. Changes.** Changes to this notice will be announced in the release notes and in the app.

  का इतना ही उपयोग है; आपके, आपके फ़ोन या आपके ग्राहकों के बारे में कुछ नहीं भेजा जाता।
